**How to make it happen:**
1. **Pitch it early.** Conference budgets are usually set a year ahead *(general)*. Before raising it with your manager, write down why you'd attend (reliability and testing practice, or robotics for ACIRS / the Electronics Fair) and roughly what it would cost.
2. **Presenting gets you funded.** A paper, industry talk, workshop or poster is the usual way to justify the trip. Deadlines for an October conference typically fall in the first half of the year *(general)*, so check ISSRE's call for papers (link in RESULTS.md) and track the deadline with `node track.mjs add manual --title "ISSRE 2027 submission" --url <cfp link> --closes <deadline>`.
3. **Earn the points yourself.** Book work flights with your own Qantas or Asia Miles number on the booking. Check your travel policy allows it; most corporate bookings do *(general)*. Points and status credits on a long-haul return can fund the personal leg.
4. **Personal extension.** Ask whether you can add leave before or after and fly home via Hong Kong → London/Dublin → Belfast. Usually you pay only the fare difference or the extra leg *(general; check your employer's policy)*. Use points for that leg.
5. **If self-funding a work conference**, part of the cost may be tax-deductible as self-education *(general)*. Ask an accountant before relying on this.

## 6. Track every entry

Promoters usually contact winners by email or phone, and the claim window can be short. Unclaimed prizes are often redrawn *(general)*, so a missed email can mean a lost prize.

- Straight after entering, run `node track.mjs add <id> --by <name>`. Ids are in [COMPETITIONS.md](COMPETITIONS.md). For skill competitions, add `--answer "…"`. For purchase competitions, add `--ref <receipt or policy no.>` and keep the receipt: proof of purchase is normally needed to claim *(general)*.
- Enter from **one dedicated email address** and record it with `--email`. Check that inbox **and its spam folder** at least weekly.
- `npm run due` lists the entries whose results should now be known, wins still waiting to be claimed, and A/B competitions closing within 3 days that nobody has entered.
- Record outcomes with `node track.mjs status <id> won --claim-by <date>`, or `not-won`. `ENTRIES.md` is the at-a-glance view.
